# Favorites

You can favorite many items in Linear — including issues, projects, views, documents, initiatives, cycles, labels, teams, customers, dashboards, pull requests, and releases — to keep them easy to access from the sidebar.

![Linear app showing a doc that is favourited](https://webassets.linear.app/images/ornj730p/production/21619d150ec1a00878e780cf206c6dc1dc875844-1564x1018.png?w=1440&q=95&auto=format&dpr=2)

## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/docs/favorites.html#overview)

Favorites are personal shortcuts which appear in your sidebar.

KeyboardMouseCommand menu

`O` then `F` to open favorites

`Alt` + `F` to toggle favorite on the focused item

* Click the star next to issue, project, label, and custom views to add or remove a favorite
* Click on favorites from the sidebar
* Hover over a favorite then click `X` to remove it

`favorite` to favorite or unfavorite issue

`open favorite` to open favorite

## Basics[⁠](https://linear.app/docs/favorites.html#basics)

### Applicable views[⁠](https://linear.app/docs/favorites.html#applicable-views)

If a view can be favorited, you’ll see the outline of a star in the top bar next to the view name. You can favorite the following views in Linear:

* Issues
* Users
* [Projects](https://linear.app/docs/projects.html)
* [Specific Cycles](https://linear.app/docs/use-cycles.html)
* [Custom Views](https://linear.app/docs/custom-views.html)
* [Label views](https://linear.app/docs/custom-views.html#label-views)
* [Default views](https://linear.app/docs/default-views.html): all issues, active issues, backlog, board, current cycles, upcoming cycles.

Some views cannot be favorited, like the view of another user’s issues that you see after clicking their name. Instead, you can create a custom view with filters to see the same issues, and add this to your favorites instead.

### Add favorites[⁠](https://linear.app/docs/favorites.html#add-favorites)

Select the star in the page header to add a favorite. You can also open the command menu and search for `favorite`. Favorite actions may not appear in every contextual menu.

### Create folders[⁠](https://linear.app/docs/favorites.html#create-folders)

To create a folder for favorites, hover over favorites and click the icon to the right to create a new folder, or create from the ⭐ icon menu. You can give it a name of your choosing and easily drag any existing favorites in or out of the folder.

### Access favorites[⁠](https://linear.app/docs/favorites.html#access-favorites)

Once you add your first favorite, the *Favorites* section appears in your sidebar above *Your Teams*. You can access favorites by clicking on them from the sidebar or searching for them in the [command menu](https://linear.app/docs/conceptual-model.html). The shortcut `O` then `F` will open a menu from which you can open a favorite, too.

### Remove favorite[⁠](https://linear.app/docs/favorites.html#remove-favorite)

Hover over the favorite in the sidebar and click on the `X` to remove it. You can add it again later by returning to the item or view and selecting the star.
