## Questions and answers

What’s included in the Linear startup program?

The Linear startup program is designed to help early-stage startups discover a better, faster way to build and ship great products. Startups affiliated with [a partner in Linear’s startup program](https://linear.app/startups/partners.html#partners) receive free access to Linear’s [Business plan](https://linear.app/pricing.html).

Who is eligible?

To redeem the offer you must be affiliated with [an official Linear partner](https://linear.app/startups/partners.html#partners). The offer is only valid for non-paying Linear users with less than 50 employees.

I’m not affiliated with a Linear partner, can I still apply?

Only companies affiliated with an official Linear partner can participate in the program. Linear does, however, offer a generous [free plan](https://linear.app/pricing.html) that includes all major features, allows unlimited workspace members, and 250 issues (plus unlimited archived issues). If your investor is not part of the program and you think they should be, [ask them to apply](https://linear.app/startups/partners.html#apply).

Our company is larger than 50 employees, can I still apply?

The Linear startup program is designed for early-stage startups. Only companies with fewer than 50 employees are eligible to participate. If your company is larger, [please contact our sales team](https://linear.app/contact/sales.html).

What if I don’t see my partner?

You can see all participating partners of the Linear startup program [on this page](https://linear.app/startups/partners.html#partners). If your partner is not part of the program, you can ask them to [apply to become a partner](https://linear.app/startups/partners.html#apply).

Can I ask my investor or accelerator to apply?

Yes. Ask your investor or accelerator to [fill out this form](https://linear.app/startups/partners.html#apply) to apply to become a Linear partner and we’ll get back to you.

How do I redeem through a partner?

Follow the redemption link provided by your partner, or enter your partner code when prompted. You need to be an admin of your Linear workspace and logged into your account to redeem the offer.

What happens when the free period ends?

When your discount period ends, your Business subscription automatically renews at the standard rate unless you switch plans or cancel beforehand.

I have more questions

Please [contact our support team](https://linear.app/contact/support.html).

How can I apply?

To apply, ask your partner for their dedicated partner link and follow it to redeem the offer. If your team already uses Linear, you’ll need to be a workspace admin to redeem.
