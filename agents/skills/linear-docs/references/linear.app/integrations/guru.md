# Connect Linear and Guru for instant, AI-powered answers

## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/guru.html#overview)

Guru’s integration with Linear seamlessly incorporates your Linear knowledge and projects into Guru’s knowledge management platform, enabling your team to easily discover and access critical project information without disrupting their workflow or relying on colleagues.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/guru.html#how-it-works)

This powerful combination allows your team to harness Linear’s purpose-built tools for planning and building products while leveraging Guru’s AI-powered search capabilities.

By bringing Linear’s data directly into Guru, your team gains instant access to crucial project information, issues, and roadmaps without interrupting their workflow. The integration offers AI-powered searches within Linear, enabling quick discovery of relevant information across projects, issues, and product roadmaps.

Key benefits include:
Enhanced Accessibility: Instantly search and access Linear’s data from any platform, powered by Guru’s AI technology.

Streamlined Collaboration: Access a single source of truth across platforms, reducing knowledge silos and enhancing team efficiency.

Continuous Knowledge Updates: As your team interacts with both systems, Guru captures and organizes important information, ensuring your knowledge base stays current.
Scalable Project Management: The integration scales with your organization, maintaining ease of use even as your project repository grows.

Chrome Extension Integration: Access instant AI answers, content recommendations, and connected apps directly within Linear, further streamlining your workflow.
