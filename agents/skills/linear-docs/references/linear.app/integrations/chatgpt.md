# Connect ChatGPT deep research to Linear

![Linear connector in deep research on ChatGPT](https://webassets.linear.app/images/ornj730p/production/39aa648c3aff56692a57f7286c80e005e4a6c1ed-2502x1710.png?q=95&auto=format&dpr=2)

![Linear connector in deep research on ChatGPT](https://webassets.linear.app/images/ornj730p/production/39aa648c3aff56692a57f7286c80e005e4a6c1ed-2502x1710.png?q=95&auto=format&dpr=2)

## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/chatgpt.html#overview)

Connect Linear to ChatGPT through deep research to reference internal sources & pull in real-time context—keeping existing user-level permissions.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/chatgpt.html#how-it-works)

The ChatGPT Connector for Linear helps you explore and reference your team’s work while staying in the flow of research. When used in Deep Research Mode, it enhances ChatGPT’s ability to surface Linear issues, projects, and discussions relevant to your current context.

## Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/chatgpt.html#configure)

Connect Linear to ChatGPT from the [connector settings.](https://chatgpt.com/#settings/ConnectorSettings)
Once connected, you can add Linear as a source when using deep research mode in ChatGPT. The Linear connector is available in deep research for Plus & Pro users (excl. EEA, CH, UK) and Team, Enterprise & Edu users.
