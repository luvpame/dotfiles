## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/viasocket.html#overview)

Integrate viaSocket with Linear to enhance project management efficiency. This integration enables the automation of project creation and updates, customer management, issue tracking and resolution, and quick searching of projects or issues by their ID. By minimising manual tasks, it helps teams stay organized, optimize workflows, and concentrate on what matters most—delivering projects effectively.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/viasocket.html#how-it-works)

viaSocket is an AI-driven workflow automation tool designed to simplify communication between different applications by connecting them at the API level. With viaSocket, users can seamlessly integrate Linear into their workflows, eliminating manual tasks and ensuring smooth data synchronisation across systems.

Users can create and update projects, issues, and customers effortlessly.

Additionally, viaSocket enables advanced data retrieval by allowing users to find customers, projects, and issues by their ID, ensuring quick access to relevant information without navigating through multiple interfaces.

By leveraging viaSocket’s Linear integration, teams can enhance productivity by automating repetitive tasks, improving issue tracking, and maintaining up-to-date project information.
