## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/teamplify.html#overview)

Teamplify is an ultimate development team productivity suite that helps to analyze and improve team collaboration. It integrates with Linear, GitHub, Slack, Zoom, Google Workspace, and many others.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/teamplify.html#how-it-works)

Teamplify provides multiple tools that help teams achieve their best. These tools can be used separately or work together to create synergy:

* Team Analytics: you can often get a sense of how your team is doing by simply looking at the data. It becomes much easier when all data is in one place - issues, commits, PRs, meetings, vacations, and holidays.
* Smart Daily Standup: virtual standups in Slack or Mattermost.
* Effortless Time Tracking – time spent on issues is calculated automatically based on issue history and work schedule.
* 360º feedback – collect feedback from teammates and see it in the context of Team Analytics.
* Time Off Management – vacations, sick days, national holidays, and more – integrated with all the other features.

## Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/teamplify.html#configure)

You’ll need admin rights in both Linear and Teamplify to connect the integration. Go to your Teamplify workspace, select [Integrations](https://teamplify.com/sign-in/?next=/organization/integrations/linear), click Linear, and follow the instructions.
