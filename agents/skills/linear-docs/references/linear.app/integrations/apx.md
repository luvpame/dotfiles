## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/apx.html#overview)

APX is a proactive, always-on, expert engineer who continuously monitors your production systems to detect critical issues and provide clear, actionable remediation steps right inside your existing workflow. Think of APX as your personal staff-engineer who proactively watches over your system and never sleeps.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/apx.html#how-it-works)

Designed to integrate effortlessly with Linear, DataDog, Sentry, Postgres, Slack, and MySQL, APX’s intelligent agents scan your critical systems, watching for alerts, performance bottlenecks, reliability risks, error spikes, and costly logging patterns. When an issue is detected, you can create a detailed Linear issue directly through APX with root cause analysis, recommended fixes, and potential cost-saving insights. From optimizing query performance to reducing DataDog log costs, APX turns insights into actionable tasks, keeping your engineering team focused on what matters.

Once APX integrates with your existing tools and Linear, it begins to continuously monitor for critical issues across your stack. When APX detects a problem, whether it’s a slow query, an error spike, or excessive logging, click the “Create Linear Issue” button to seamlessly track and assign tasks. Here is what APX provides your team:

* 24/7 monitoring for DBA, performance, and SRE checks
* Smart issue enrichment, sharing root cause analysis and system impact
* Customizable alerts and prompts to tailor APX to your team’ workflow
* Actionable solutions with explanations and paste-ready code fixes

With our integration issues are synced to Linear, keeping your team up-to-date seamlessly without the noise. Every check includes simple, human-readable recommendations and code-level fixes you can directly paste into your IDE or AI tool of choice, maximizing developer efficiency with the “Copy for LLM” button.

## Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/apx.html#configure)

Enable the Linear integration directly within the APX platform. This integration currently supports creating and linking issues flagged by APX with its solutions.

To get started, visit your [APX Integrations Settings](https://apx.stepchange.work/integrations).
