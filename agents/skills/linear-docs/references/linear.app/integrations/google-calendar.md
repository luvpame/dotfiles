# Connect your calendar to automate your status

## **Overview**[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/google-calendar.html#overview)

The Linear integration with Google calendar allows you to connect your calendar and automate your status. If your teammates need to know whether you’re out of office, now they will.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/google-calendar.html#how-it-works)

Once connected, your Linear status will detect out of office events in Google calendar and update your status when those events are upcoming or active.

## To Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/google-calendar.html#to-configure)

Install Google Calendar from your Linear settings under [My Account > Connected Accounts.](https://linear.app/settings/account/connections.html)

Connect your account and sign into your Google account to allow access and the integration will update your Linear status with out of office notices.
