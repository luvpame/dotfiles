# Manage triage responsibility directly in Linear with Slack Round Robin Rotations

![Slack and Linear interfaces showing Pagerly integration](https://webassets.linear.app/images/ornj730p/production/549e5a0134a7b494842abf9e5e6e841933d38ae5-1334x740.png?q=95&auto=format&dpr=2)

![Slack and Linear interfaces showing Pagerly integration](https://webassets.linear.app/images/ornj730p/production/549e5a0134a7b494842abf9e5e6e841933d38ae5-1334x740.png?q=95&auto=format&dpr=2)

## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/pagerly.html#overview)

With Pagerly, you can create round robin rotations or Oncalls on Slack/ Teams. You can use the same schedule to assign a triage responsibility on Linear .

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/pagerly.html#how-it-works)

Create your Rotations or Oncalls (sync from Pagerduty, Opsgenie, JSM) on Slack and Microsoft Teams.

With Pagerly Linear Integration, you can assign and sync this schedule with triage responsibility schedule on Linear .

Triage Responsibility on Linear helps you assign tickets to based on a schedule. It is available on our Business and Enterprise plans which enables you to handle incoming requests. You can connect your Linear Team with Pagerly within Slack.

## Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/pagerly.html#configure)

Connect Linear and Pagerly via Slack per the following [setup guide.](https://docs.pagerly.io/how-to-work-with-pagerly/linear-integration)
