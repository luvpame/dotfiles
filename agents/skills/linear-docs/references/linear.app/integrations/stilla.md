## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/stilla.html#overview)

Stilla connects meeting notes, Slack conversations, and docs to your Linear issues. Mention **@stilla** in an issue to bring in relevant context, clarify decisions, or ask it to take action—like drafting a PR or breaking down a task.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/stilla.html#how-it-works)

Stilla plugs into every stage of your workflow—whether you’re chatting in Slack, speaking in a remote or in-person meeting, or typing directly in Stilla. It listens and understands the context of your conversations which it can then use to manage your work in Linear.

From Linear, mention **@stilla** to get relevant context about an issue. For example, ask Stilla to share context on the product decision or when the issue was last discussed. You can also ask Stilla to draft pull requests, which it will create using all of its context from meetings, code, and documents.

From Stilla, you can update and manage your work in Linear. Stilla captures product decisions from recorded conversations and automatically creates and updates Linear issues.

## Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/stilla.html#configure)

If you have access to Stilla, select your avatar to navigate to Stilla’s settings page and connect to Linear.
