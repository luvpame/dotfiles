## **Overview**[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/steady.html#overview)

Steady is the zero-effort coordination platform for running high-performance teams. It distills plans & progress from tools, teams and people into hyper-personalized digests that keep everyone in sync.

Activity on Linear issues is automatically included in teammates async check-ins, alongside quick responses that fill in critical context, and summarized in AI-powered briefs. It’s the modern way to keep remote and hybrid teams aligned and informed.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/steady.html#how-it-works)

Steady makes it easy to see your team’s activity across all your tools. Once you connect Linear to Steady, issue updates from every teammate are automatically included in their smart async check-ins and available for AI-powered reports that summarize Linear progress. Less paperwork, more context, more focus time.

Issue updates also show up in Steady’s live activity stream along with all your other integrated tools. Instead of looking all over the place to piece together a picture of what’s going on, you have one-stop source of truth.

## To Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/steady.html#to-configure)

In Linear, visit Settings > Account > API. Add a new Webhook using the URL from the Linear panel in your Steady account settings.
