## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/feedovate.html#overview)

Capture feature requests, feedback, and bug reports in Feedovate and sync them with Linear. Create new Linear issues from Feedovate posts or link to existing ones, and keep statuses synced both ways so your roadmap updates automatically and customers always know what’s shipping next.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/feedovate.html#how-it-works)

Feedovate connects directly with your Linear workspace using OAuth authentication and keeps customer feedback and development progress in sync. Once connected, you can create new Linear issues from feedback posts or link existing issues to feature requests, bug reports, and customer discussions inside Feedovate.

When issues are updated in Linear, statuses automatically sync back to Feedovate so your public roadmap always reflects the latest progress. Customers can vote on ideas, leave feedback, and follow updates without needing access to Linear, while your internal comments, private issues, and sensitive project data remain securely inside your workspace.

Teams can organize feedback into boards, prioritize requests based on customer demand, and share selected Linear issues publicly through a customizable roadmap. Feedovate helps product, engineering, and customer-facing teams stay aligned while keeping customers informed throughout the development process.

## Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/feedovate.html#configure)

To connect Linear with Feedovate, go to your Feedovate workspace Dashboard -> Settings -> Integrations and select Connect to Linear. The user connecting Linear must have permission to manage integrations in Feedovate and must be able to authorize apps in the Linear workspace.

Once connected, Feedovate can sync customer feedback with Linear issues. Teams can push feedback posts to Linear, link feedback to existing Linear issues, and configure status sync rules so updates stay aligned between Feedovate and Linear. New feedback can also be automatically sent to Linear when auto-push is enabled. Linear buttons appear on feedback posts so teams can quickly create, link, unlink, and navigate between Feedovate feedback and Linear issues.
