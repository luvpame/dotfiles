## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/graph.html#overview)

Graph is your AI-powered assistant in Slack — it’s like having a knowledgeable team member who instantly understands your code repository and tickets. Just chat with it to get answers about PRs, development progress, and work tasks. Graph connects to Linear (and GitHub) to give you the insights you need.

Get scheduled insights, like a team progress summary or an overview of repo activity. Or, ask Graph ad-hoc when you need answers on the fly, like getting caught up after being OOO or drafting release notes.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/graph.html#how-it-works)

Graph integrates Linear with GitHub and your Slack workspace to provide engineering teams with instant insights without context switching. By analyzing your Linear tickets, code repositories, and development activity, Graph delivers contextual answers about project status, sprint progress, and team metrics directly in Slack. Users can interact with Graph through direct messages or by mentioning it in channels, making insights accessible wherever your team communicates.

The integration captures your team’s work activity data, extracting meaningful patterns and surfacing critical details that matter. Graph can monitor sprint progress, identify stale tickets, analyze PR activity, visualize velocity metrics, and highlight potential bottlenecks—all while maintaining SOC 2 certification and GDPR compliance. Teams can customize their profiles to receive more relevant insights about the repositories, projects, and priorities they care about most.

Graph enhances Linear’s project management capabilities by providing proactive insights that help teams see around corners. Beyond basic status tracking, Graph adds contextual understanding to your Linear data, enabling features like automated changelogs, resource allocation breakdowns, cycle time analysis, and burndown visualization. Teams can also schedule recurring insights like daily activity reports or weekly status updates, ensuring everyone stays informed without manual data gathering.

## Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/graph.html#configure)

Graph is available to install directly from the [Slack Marketplace.](https://slack.s.graphapp.ai/slack/install) During the beta period, Graph is free for all beta customers. Note that a paid Slack plan is required to use the Agent & Assistant functionality in Graph.

You’ll need Admin permissions to install apps in your Slack workspace, and on the platforms you’d like to connect (e.g. GitHub, Linear).
