## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/granola.html#overview)

Connect Linear to Granola via Zapier to turn your meeting action items into Linear issues automatically — so what you agreed to in the call lands in your engineering backlog without any copy-pasting.

Zapier is available to paid Granola users on the desktop app, and you’ll need a Zapier account.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/granola.html#how-it-works)

Once the Zap is live, every note that hits your trigger flows straight to Linear:

* Point the folder trigger at a folder like **Customer Interviews** — new notes added there create issues automatically.
* Or leave it manual and push a specific note to Zapier from the share menu when you want it in Linear.
* Each issue is populated with the note’s action items, so your backlog reflects what was actually decided in the meeting.

## Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/granola.html#configure)

1. In Zapier, create a new Zap and choose **Granola** as the trigger app.
2. Pick your trigger — **Note added to Granola folder** to fire automatically whenever a note lands in a chosen folder, or **Note shared to Zapier** to push notes by hand from the note’s share menu.
3. Add **Linear** as the action app and select **Create Issue**.
4. Map the note’s action items into the issue title and description, choose the team and project, then turn the Zap on.
