# Automatically assign SuperDuty member to triage

## **Overview**[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/superduty.html#overview)

Automatically assign to Triage the current on-call person and streamline your bug triage workflow.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/superduty.html#how-it-works)

SuperDuty helps you manage your rotations easily. It can be integrated perfectly with Linear. Automatically assign your on-duty member to Triage. SuperDuty also manage absences to make sure the right person is always assigned.
Create your Schedule (per team or per project) and choose your rotation configuration. Then add the Linear automation, choose the member you want to rotate and that’s it.
You can also configure more automations to get notifications in your preferred messaging tools (like Slack or Discord)

## To Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/superduty.html#to-configure)

Triage responsibility requires the Business or Enterprise plans of Linear.
You can connect Linear inside [SuperDuty](https://console.superduty.app/organization/integration).
