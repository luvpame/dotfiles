## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/integrately.html#overview)

Integrately is your no-code automation platform to create, search, or update issues when a certain event occurs in other apps.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/integrately.html#how-it-works)

Integrately helps you connect Linear with 1150+ applications such as Google Sheets, Notion, GitHub and many more.

With the Integrately integration for Linear, you can automate creating, updating, and commenting on issues in response to triggers. For example, your team can be notified in Notion of any issues raised or bugs encountered.

You can either select 1-click automation or build your own custom automation by selecting a Trigger and Action. Creating workflows becomes easier with Integrately due to its zero learning curve.

## Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/integrately.html#configure)

To get started for free, [create an Integrately account](https://integrately.com/integrations/linear) and connect Linear with your chosen application.
