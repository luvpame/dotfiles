## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/11ai.html#overview)

11.ai is a voice-first personal assistant built with ElevenLabs conversational AI.

Plan your work and manage your Linear issues all without touching your keyboard.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/11ai.html#how-it-works)

11.ai connects to your Linear workspace with access to [MCP](https://linear.app/docs/mcp.html) tools so it can make actions on your workspace such as searching, updating and creating issues.

## Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/11ai.html#configure)

Create an account on [11.ai](https://11.ai) and connect the Linear integration to begin interacting with your workspace. Allow access to the MCP tools when prompted.
