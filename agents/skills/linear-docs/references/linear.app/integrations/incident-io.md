## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/incident-io.html#overview)

Connect incidents to Linear issues and keep follow-ups and engineering work in sync automatically.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/incident-io.html#how-it-works)

* **Create Linear issues from incident follow-ups** with one click
* **Automatically link incidents and issues** so engineering can see why the work exists
* **Sync updates between [incident.io](http://incident.io) and Linear**, reducing manual updates
* **Keep operational and engineering work aligned**, with full incident context attached to each issue
* **Improve post-incident reviews**, with a clear picture of completed and outstanding actions

This integration helps engineering managers, SREs, and responders close the loop between firefighting and continuous improvement, without switching tools.

Connect Linear and [incident.io](http://incident.io/) to make follow-ups actionable, visible, and easy for engineering teams to execute—long after the incident is resolved.

## Triage responsibility[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/incident-io.html#triage-responsibility)

[Triage responsibility](https://linear.app/docs/triage.html#triage-responsibility) is a feature available on our Business and Enterprise plans which enables you to handle incoming requests. Once triage responsibility is set, there is the option to connect your incident.io schedule to automate the rotation of first responders.

## Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/incident-io.html#configure)

Adding Linear to incident.io is simple. Just log into your incident.io web app, go to *Settings > Integrations* and hit *Connect* next to Linear. Log into your Linear account and you’re all set.

Incident.io is also available as an [MCP server](https://linear.app/docs/connect-mcp-servers-to-linear-agent.html) for use with Linear Agent
