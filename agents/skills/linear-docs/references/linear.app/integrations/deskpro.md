## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/deskpro.html#overview)

View and update your Linear issues linked with Deskpro tickets to streamline communication with users.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/deskpro.html#how-it-works)

* Link Issues with Tickets: Boost your team’s communication by linking issues to tickets right within the app. It’s a neat feature that gives agents easy access to information from their Linear instance, streamlining communication with your users.
* Create Issues: Create new issues easily based on the info you’ve got from your users. It’s all about convenience.
* View Issues: Stay in the loop with the latest changes to your issues by taking a peek at their properties.
* Edit Issues: Got new and useful info from your users? Great, just tweak your issue properties.
* Add Comments: Add more context to your issues using Linear’s handy comment feature. It lets you drop in additional info whenever you need to.
