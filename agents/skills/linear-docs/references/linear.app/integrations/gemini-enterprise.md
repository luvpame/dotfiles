## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/gemini-enterprise.html#overview)

Use Gemini Enterprise to search, summarize, and analyze data across all your work apps to get instant answers and insights right where you work.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/gemini-enterprise.html#how-it-works)

With the Linear connector for Gemini, you can create new issues, or pull in data from existing issues and projects without leaving Gemini. Surface answers from your Linear workspace in seconds.

## Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/gemini-enterprise.html#configure)

1. Navigate to [Gemini Enterprise](https://business.gemini.google/)
2. Click on the data source icon to display the full list of available connectors.
3. Select the Linear Connector
4. Authenticate with your Linear workspace
5. Your account is now successfully connected. You can begin using Gemini to retrieve and work with the data you need.
