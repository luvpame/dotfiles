## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/monkedo.html#overview)

Monkedo is a no-code solution to help you integrate with over 400 applications in minutes.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/monkedo.html#how-it-works)

Monkedo is a platform to integration your favourite applications and services into Linear through manual, scheduled, webhook or email-based triggers and automations. You can create automations effortlessly using a simple drag and drop editor.

## Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/monkedo.html#configure)

Create your [Monkedo account](https://app.monkedo.com/automations/create) and choose your chosen applications and triggers you want to send to Linear.
