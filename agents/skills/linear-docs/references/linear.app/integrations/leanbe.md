## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/leanbe.html#overview)

Seamlessly bridge your product planning with execution. This integration ensures your product strategy translates directly into actionable tasks, keeping teams aligned and productive.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/leanbe.html#how-it-works)

Imagine a bridge between idea conception and execution. Once connected, Leanbe becomes that bridge for your product ideas, linking them seamlessly to Linear issues. No more manual transfers or data mismatches; everything syncs smoothly and effortlessly.

Real-time notifications act as your personal assistants. You’re instantly alerted about any changes, ensuring that you’re always in the know without constantly juggling between platforms.

Your team can easily participate in the ideation process, provide valuable feedback, and understand the broader product vision. With everyone on the same page, you can expect more cohesive teamwork and better product outcomes.

## Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/leanbe.html#configure)

As a Linear admin, connect via your Leanbe dashboard under Settings > Integrations and follow the [instructions here.](https://help.productmix.com/docs/how-to-integrate-ProductMix-with-linear/)
