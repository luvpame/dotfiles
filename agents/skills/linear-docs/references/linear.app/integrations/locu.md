## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/locu.html#overview)

The next-generation productivity app built for makers. Combine your tasks, notes, and focus sessions into one unified workspace designed for maximum execution and deep work..

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/locu.html#how-it-works)

Locu integrates with Linear to bring your assigned work into a focused, actionable flow. Import all issues assigned to you for the current cycle with one click, then break them down into smaller steps, checklists, and private notes that live alongside the issue. Everything stays in 2‑way sync: updates in Linear appear immediately in Locu, and changes you make in Locu are reflected back in Linear.

Locu is built for deep work. Start a focus session on a single issue, reduce distractions, and keep key context right where you need it. Time is tracked automatically as you work in sessions, giving you a realistic picture of effort. Linear remains your team-level issue tracker, while Locu supports day-to-day execution with clarity and steady momentum.

## Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/locu.html#configure)

In Locu, go to [Settings → Integrations → Linear](locu://preferences/integrations/linear), then click Enable. You will be redirected to a Linear authorization page. If prompted, log in to your Linear account. Click Allow to grant Locu access to your Linear workspace.
After authorization, you will be redirected back to the Locu website, confirming the successful connection. Your integration is now ready to use.
