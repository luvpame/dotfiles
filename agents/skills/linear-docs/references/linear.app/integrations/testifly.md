## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/testifly.html#overview)

The Testifly integration for Linear allows you to connect your engineering workflow directly to Testifly, an AI-powered testing platform built around the generate → execute → heal → adapt cycle process. It allows teams to turn Linear issues into automated web tests, automatically identify work that is ready for QA, and use AI-assisted workflows to generate, execute, maintain, and adapt tests as the product changes — helping teams reduce manual QA effort, improve coverage, and keep tests aligned with ongoing development.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/testifly.html#how-it-works)

The Linear integration brings Testifly directly into your team’s workflow as an AI testing agent that works alongside your existing development process. Teams can connect Linear to Testifly and interact with testing workflows directly from their Linear-driven work, making it easier to turn product changes into actionable testing tasks without switching between multiple tools.

The integration continuously monitors Linear issues and workflow updates to help teams stay on top of testing needs as the product evolves. When issues are updated or moved into a ready-for-testing state, Testifly can review the changes, identify potential testing impact, and suggest whether existing tests should be updated or new tests should be created. This helps QA and product teams keep coverage aligned with active development while reducing manual review work.

Testifly also supports AI-driven testing workflows built around its generate → execute → heal → adapt cycle. Teams can use Linear issues as the starting point for generating automated tests, executing them, maintaining them as the application changes, and adapting coverage over time. The result is a more connected workflow between product delivery and testing, with less manual effort required to keep tests relevant and up to date.

## Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/testifly.html#configure)

From inside your Testifly, navigate to the [Settings > Integrations](https://app.testifly.io/settings/integrations) page and select the **Connect As Agent** option. Testifly will redirect you to the official Linear authorization Log in to Linear as an admin and grant Testifly permission to access your workspace.

And you are ready to go!
