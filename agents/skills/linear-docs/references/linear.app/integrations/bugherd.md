## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/bugherd.html#overview)

Make it easy for your clients and team to report bugs and mark up changes for their website, then combine the ease of BugHerd feedback with the project management capabilities you love about Linear.

Integrating BugHerd with Linear gives your team an easy way to manage, track and resolve bugs and issues without needing your clients to have Linear access. BugHerd provides the technical information (browser, operating system, web page URL) and a screenshot, and turns it into an actionable Linear ticket. Choose when and how tasks are sent from BugHerd to Linear.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/bugherd.html#how-it-works)

Combine the easy website feedback from BugHerd + the streamlined software development process from Linear.

Link your BugHerd account to Linear to send website bugs submitted by your team and clients in BugHerd through to Linear for your development team to resolve.

Integrating BugHerd with Linear gives your team an easy way to manage, track and resolve bugs and issues without needing your clients to have access to your teams Linear account. The integration allows you to choose when and how tasks are sent from BugHerd to Linear.

## Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/bugherd.html#configure)

Integrate BugHerd with Linear in 3 easy steps:

1. Link your BugHerd project to Linear from the Integrations page in BugHerd.
2. Select which Linear Team and Issue you’d like to send BugHerd tasks to.
3. Choose where and how you send BugHerd tasks to Linear, and what information you want updated and when. You’re in control.

You’ll need to have current plans for BugHerd and Linear to be able to integrate them. You will need to have a project set up in BugHerd. You’ll also need to be a manager in BugHerd. If you’d like to see how BugHerd works and how it integrates with Linear, start a 14-day free BugHerd trial here: [https://bugherd.com](https://bugherd.com/)
