## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/linage.html#overview)

Linage connects to your Linear workspace and automatically turns cycle data into a plain-English weekly engineering brief. Instead of digging through dashboards, engineering managers get a narrative report covering velocity, completion rate, carry-overs, bottlenecks, and recommended next steps — ready to forward to leadership in under a minute.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/linage.html#how-it-works)

Once connected, Linage uses read-only OAuth to sync your completed cycles, issues, assignees, estimates, labels, and state history from Linear. It computes engineering metrics on the server — including velocity trend, completion rate, carry-over count, cycle time, bottleneck stage, workload distribution and bug ratio — then sends those metrics, along with limited at-risk issue context when needed, to AI to write a structured narrative report.

Each report includes a health score, executive summary, highlights, risk analysis, and actionable recommendations. Reports can be viewed in the Linage dashboard, shared via a secure link, exported as PDF, or delivered automatically to Slack or email on a weekly schedule. You can generate a report on demand for any completed cycle, or let Linage run it automatically every Monday.

Linage never writes to Linear. It cannot create, edit, or delete issues. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest, and you can disconnect Linear at any time from Settings.

Free accounts include 3 lifetime reports; paid plans unlock unlimited reports, Slack delivery, and extended history.

## Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/linage.html#configure)

Setup takes about five minutes and does not require Linear workspace admin rights.

1. Sign up at <https://linage.ailaile.tech> and create your Linage account.
2. On onboarding step 1, click “Connect Linear.” You will be redirected to Linear to approve read-only OAuth access (issues, cycles, teams, and users).
3. On step 2, select which Linear teams you want included in reports.
4. On step 3, choose how you want reports delivered: Slack, email, or view-only in the dashboard. Slack delivery requires connecting a Slack workspace and selecting a channel (Pro plan).
5. Linage generates your first report from the most recent completed cycle.

To update teams, delivery schedule, or re-authorize Linear later, go to [Settings](https://linage.ailaile.tech/settings) in your Linage dashboard.
