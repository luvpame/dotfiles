## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/ifttt.html#overview)

Automate, save time, and get more done with Linear and IFTTT. Easily connect Linear to 1000+ apps and devices to create time-saving workflows - no code required.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/ifttt.html#how-it-works)

IFTTT links two or more apps with automations called Applets. Choose a trigger, pick an action, and customize it to your needs. You can enable 100,000s of ready-made Applets or create your own in minutes.

**Key Features**

* Connect Linear to 1000+ apps, services, and devices: Integrate with Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, task management, calendar, and communication platforms with Linear triggers and actions.
* Use IFTTT on the go: Our popular mobile apps for iOS and Android allow you to connect Linear to widgets, custom notifications to your phone, and location-based triggers.
* Increase your productivity: Automations are easy to set up, personalize, and you can get started with your first Applet in minutes.

## Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/ifttt.html#configure)

* Click **Enable** to access the Linear service page on IFTTT or go to [IFTTT for Linear](https://ifttt.com/linear)
* Click **Connect Linear** to connect your account to IFTTT
* Click and enable any of the published Applets available or click [**Create**](https://ifttt.com/create) to build your own
