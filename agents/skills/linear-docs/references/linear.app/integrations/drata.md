## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/drata.html#overview)

Integrate Linear with Drata’s security, risk, and compliance automation platform to ensure you are getting and staying compliant while tackling your day-to-day work.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/drata.html#how-it-works)

The integration inspects your company task tracking system to determine if security issues are being tagged and prioritized accordingly. This ensures your company tracks, assigns, and prioritizes security deficiencies according to their severity.

## Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/drata.html#configure)

1. Select *Connections* on the lower left corner of Drata
2. In the search bar type *Linear*
3. Press *Connect*
4. The slide-out panel will provide step-by-step instructions

Note: If you do not use “Security”as the label within Linear to categorize tickets as security issues, be sure to update the ‘Security Label’ within the panel.
