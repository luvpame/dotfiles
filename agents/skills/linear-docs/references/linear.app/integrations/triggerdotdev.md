## Overview[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/triggerdotdev.html#overview)

Easily sync your services with Linear to automate creating and updating issues, projects, cycles and more.

## How it works[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/triggerdotdev.html#how-it-works)

The Trigger.dev integration simplifies the process of creating long-running background jobs that use Linear, directly within your codebase.

Manage issues, projects, cycles and much more. Connect to other services, create jobs on a schedule, or add delays (of up to a year), all from your serverless app.

To authenticate, you have the option to use OAuth or Personal API keys, depending on your preference. Trigger.dev can subscribe to Linear’s webhooks, enabling you to trigger any events and perform any tasks that are possible with Linear’s API.

Frameworks currently supported:
• Next.js
• Remix
• Astro
• Express
• NestJS

## Configure[⁠](https://linear.app/integrations/triggerdotdev.html#configure)

To get started, please check Trigger.dev’s [documentation](https://trigger.dev/docs/integrations/apis/linear), you can either use OAuth or a Personal API Key.
