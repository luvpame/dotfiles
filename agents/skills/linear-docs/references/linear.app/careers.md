## On a quest for quality

We believe that there is a lost art of building software. A craftsmanship that yields products of an exceptional, almost magical, quality.

It’s difficult to describe this quality with words and impossible to measure it in numbers, but you can feel it when it’s there. You know it when you experience it. To bring back this level of quality, we are building a tool that empowers product teams to do their best work. A tool with unparalleled speed, focus, and design.

Building this tool requires a team of talented individuals who care deeply about the quality of their work, no matter if it’s engineering, design, or customer support. We are looking for people who share our passion for software craftsmanship and getting even the smallest details right. If that sounds like you, please reach out and join us on our quest.

## How we think and work

Linear’s mission is to inspire and accelerate builders. To make it happen, we operate based on a set of core principles that keep us focused.

* 01.Ship early

  Simplify and ship smaller.
* 02.Build with users

  Listen carefully and understand their problems.
* 03.Know what good looks like

  Find and create good things. Know what needs to change to make it better.
* 04.Think in principles

  Not in playbooks.
* 05.Build things that last

  Put care into the foundation. Don’t make it a future person’s problem.
* 06.Avoid side quests

  Don’t fix every problem. Avoid adding unnecessary process or documents.
* 07.Say it as it is

  Get to the point and assume good intentions.
* 08.Keep the team small, do more with less

  We build better with fewer people. Wear multiple hats.
* 09.Hire the best people

  The best people hire the best people. Help recruit.
* 10.Create fans

  Build a community of people who use, love, and defend the product.
* 11.Fully remote

  The freedom to work remotely allows for more meaningful work.
