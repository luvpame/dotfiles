## Delegate, automate, and orchestrate work

Choose from a variety of AI agents and start
­delegating work, from code generation to other
­technical tasks.

![](https://linear.app/cdn-cgi/imagedelivery/fO02fVwohEs9s9UHFwon6A/3b27a057-fafc-4d03-8073-6e6043626200/f=auto,fit=scale-down,metadata=none)

### Fast, focused, and reliable. Just like the rest of your team.

Agents are full members of your Linear workspace. Assign them to issues, add them to projects, or @mention them in comments.

![](https://linear.app/cdn-cgi/imagedelivery/fO02fVwohEs9s9UHFwon6A/f704ba59-be6d-45ef-20cf-d29f147c5b00/f=auto,fit=scale-down,metadata=none)

![](https://linear.app/cdn-cgi/imagedelivery/fO02fVwohEs9s9UHFwon6A/d737431f-cace-4296-ed65-bd75c5ddb000/f=auto,fit=scale-down,metadata=none)

### Delegate issues, but not accountability

When an issue gets delegated to an agent, the human user remains the primary assignee, while the agent is added as a contributor.

![](https://linear.app/cdn-cgi/imagedelivery/fO02fVwohEs9s9UHFwon6A/0e75e892-8b25-4541-0b01-810f38bf2e00/f=auto,fit=scale-down,metadata=none)

![](https://linear.app/cdn-cgi/imagedelivery/fO02fVwohEs9s9UHFwon6A/6a3d1120-4dee-4994-dcc2-443680a02300/f=auto,fit=scale-down,metadata=none)

### Stay in the loop every step of the way

Agents act on your behalf, but never in the dark. Understand every change they make at a glance, or inspect the underlying reasoning.

![](https://linear.app/cdn-cgi/imagedelivery/fO02fVwohEs9s9UHFwon6A/bc114347-cc3b-40c6-3bd5-31e52c285900/f=auto,fit=scale-down,metadata=none)

![](https://linear.app/cdn-cgi/imagedelivery/fO02fVwohEs9s9UHFwon6A/e9b0b10a-f256-41f8-a564-05e531669700/f=auto,fit=scale-down,metadata=none)

### Orchestrate tasks at scale

Agents can work across multiple issues simultaneously. Select tasks, assign them to an agent, and watch work move forward.
