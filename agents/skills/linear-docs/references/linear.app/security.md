# Safe, secure, and private.

Everything in Linear is designed to keep your work safe and secure. Because your business is nobody else’s
business.

## Enterprise-grade security

Peace of mind-as-a-service. Linear is built with best-in-class security practices to keep your work safe and secure at every layer. This includes state-of-the-art encryption, safe and reliable infrastructure partners, and independently verified security controls.

#### SOC 2 compliance

Linear undergoes regular Service Organization Controls audits (SOC 2 Type II). [Request SOC 2 →](https://trust.linear.app/)

#### GDPR compliance

Committed to compliance with Europe’s General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

#### HIPAA compliance

Privacy and security measures to ensure that Protected Health Information (PHI) is appropriately safeguarded. [Request BAA →](https://linear.app/contact/sales%3Fmessage=Our%20company%20is%20subject%20to%20HIPAA%20compliance%20and%20we%20are%20interested%20in%20entering%20into%20a%20Business%20Associate%20Agreement%20(BAA)%20with%20Linear..html)

#### ISO 27001 certified

Linear has achieved ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification for its information security management system. [Request ISO →](https://trust.linear.app/)

#### App approvals

Control which third-party applications have access to your Linear workspace.

#### Audit logs

Audit logs automatically keep track of important workspace events over the last 3 months.

#### Admin controls

Restrict access to system-level privileges.

## Identity management

Keep an eye on your work. And keep other eyes out. Linear offers multiple ways to login as well as options to restrict login methods for added security.

#### Single sign-on (SSO)

Authenticate into Linear using Google SSO or login via email with email codes.

#### Passkeys

Secure and fast login without having to rely on passwords.

#### SAML

Linear offers SAML via most identity providers (Okta, OneLogin, LastPass, Auth0, Bitium, and more).

#### SCIM

Provision and deprovision users in Linear through your identity provider.

#### Domain claiming

Add an extra layer of security and keep your teammates in the right workspace.

#### Login restrictions

Restrict login to specific methods for added security (Google SSO or email logins).

#### IP restrictions

Restrict workspace access to your private network.

## Privacy

Designed to protect your workyour work.
Privacy first, second, and third. Choose what to share and with whom.

#### Private teams

Create private teams for work that should only be accessed by certain workspace members.

#### Guest accounts

Invite contractors, external stakeholders, or other members with limited workspace access.

#### Data encryption

Linear forces HTTPS on all connections and encrypts data in-transit with TLS 1.2. All data at-rest is secured using AES 256-bit encryption.

#### Multi-region hosting

Choose to store your data in the European Union or the United States when creating a Linear workspace.
