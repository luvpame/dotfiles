Linear can help you manage [unplanned work](https://linear.app/blog/planning-for-unplanned-work.html) - like bugs and feature requests - efficiently. This video covers setting up and managing Triage, integrating with support tools like Slack, and using custom views to organize and prioritize issues.

[Learn how the Linear team manages unplanned work.](https://linear.app/now/cx-in-linear.html#stage-1:-intake)

[Triage](https://linear.app/docs/triage.html) is your home for managing unplanned work. It works as a central inbox for incoming, unplanned work.

Triage is on the team level. It’s helpful for teams that handle a high volume of inbound reports or requests and where those inbound issues often require qualifying before they are assigned and worked on.

* Engineering teams use triage to ensure that all inbound bug reports contain the necessary reproduction steps and device information for an engineer to effectively resolve them
* Product teams use it to [sort customer requests](https://linear.app/now/continuous-planning-in-linear.html) into existing and upcoming projects, labels, and teams, and move anything else to the backlog
* Data teams use it to field and prioritize query requests from internal teammates

If Triage makes sense for your team, enable it in your team settings, then:

* Set up [triage responsibility](https://linear.app/docs/triage.html#triage-responsibility) to notify a teammate whenever an issue enters triage
* Activate [Triage Intelligence](https://linear.app/docs/triage.html#triage-intelligence) and configure [Triage Rules](https://linear.app/docs/triage.html#triage-rules) to expedite and inform the triage process
* Create [issue templates](https://linear.app/docs/issue-templates.html) to standardize bug report and customer request formatting and details

#### Issue intake[⁠](https://linear.app/learn/managing-unplanned-work.html#issue-intake)

Technically, triage issues are simply issues with the *triage* status, however, [integrations with support platforms](https://linear.app/docs/triage.html#support-integrations) allow you to create and sync Linear issues directly from their source.

Use [customer requests](https://linear.app/docs/customer-requests.html) to link customer attributes and quotes to issues as they’re created.

Some of these integrations will automatically sync statuses across platforms. For example, when a Linear issue linked to an Intercom ticket is resolved, [the Intercom ticket is re-opened](https://linear.app/docs/intercom.html#re-open-conversations) to flag to the support team that a follow up is needed.

Use AI tools in Linear to [accelerate your bug intake and triage workflow](https://youtu.be/B3uHgSV6ocA?si=xtUxslpv4XImuZSw).

> *From Slack, try “@Linear, create an issue for this bug report and assign it to my coding agent.”*

#### Custom views[⁠](https://linear.app/learn/managing-unplanned-work.html#custom-views)

Use custom views to manage issues as they flow into your workflow and backlog. Some filter and display settings worth trying:

* Urgent, unresolved bugs: Label: Bug; Status type: Triage, Backlog, Unstarted, Started; Due date: Overdue, 1 week from now
* Popular customer requests: Label: Feature request; Sort by: Request count

> *Ask Linear, “Look at bugs that were triaged in the last week and surface any patterns.”*

> *Ask Linear, “What are some of the most popular and impactful customer requests this quarter?”*
